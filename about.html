About

Timer matches the same proportion of break to work as Pomodoro, but offers flexibilty to take a break when you choose, rather than at fixed intervals.

As someone who doesn't take enough breaks and also doesn't like stopping in the middle of something, I made this to help my brain do good thinking.

AI has been used for almost all the coding. Not the logo though. That's just an M on top of another upside down M.
