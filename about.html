About

Timer matches the same proportion of break to work as Pomodoro, but offers flexibilty to take a break when you choose, rather than at fixed intervals.

As someone who doesn't take enough breaks and also doesn't like stopping in the middle of something, I made this to help me let my brain relax a little.

AI has been used for almost all the coding. Not the logo though. As surpsiing as that is with how complex it is.
